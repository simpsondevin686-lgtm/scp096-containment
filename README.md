# SCP-096 Containment

## How to Play

### Mobile (Touch)
- **Move:** Use the left on-screen joystick.
- **Look:** Drag on the screen.
- **Close Eyes:** Tap the **CLOSE EYES** button.
- **Look Back:** Tap the **LOOK BACK** button to turn around.
- **Chat:** Tap **CHAT** to open the message input.
- **Buy / Fire Stun Gun:** Tap the **BUY** button (costs 50 coins), then tap **FIRE** when active.

### PC (Keyboard & Mouse)
- **Move:** WASD or Arrow Keys.
- **Look:** Move the mouse or use your laptop's touchpad/mouse pad (click the screen to lock the pointer).
- **Turn Around:** Press **Space** to flip your view 180°.
- **Close/Open Eyes:** Press **E**.
- **Buy Stun Gun:** Press **B**.
- **Fire Stun Gun:** Press **F**.
- **Chat:** Press **C** to open chat, type your message, and press **Enter** to send and close it.

> **Important:** Do not look directly at SCP-096's face. While closing your eyes *before* looking at him will keep you safe, closing them *after* he has been triggered will not save you.
